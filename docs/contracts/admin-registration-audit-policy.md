# Admin registration operation audit and correction policy (#101)

This is the audit and correction contract for admin registration writes under [issue #63](admin-registration-operations.md). The delivered commands and the history reader are described below; anything still marked as proposed is not implemented. The November 2026 MVP proposal remains **DRAFT pending organizer validation**.

## Current persistence vs proposed behavior

- #62 stores participant identity (name, optional email/stage name), event registrations (optional event-scoped folio), and same-event activity registrations. These persistence fields do not yet enforce the private registration inputs agreed in #58: required first name, first last name, email and phone; optional second last name, AKA, country, city, Instagram, level and birth date. Participant identity can be shared across event registrations. #62 does not provide search or admin writes.
- #61 stores `pending_payment` (default), `confirmed`, and `voided`. A confirmed row requires `approved_payment`, `admin_cash` or `admin_complimentary` (#263) plus `confirmed_at`; pending and voided rows have neither. These are row constraints, not transition authority, audit history, or payment/refund rules.
- Admin authentication audit concerns sign-in, sessions, and access decisions; it is **not** registration operation audit. All event-scoped administrators (not judges) may use bounded corrections and read operation history for their authorized event. Server-side session, role, and event-scope guards and command-specific transition checks apply to every command. The history reader is delivered (#104, see **Registration history (#104)** below); the retention mechanism is pending (#307).
- The audit table, the admin commands that write it and the history reader below are delivered. Requirements that a command does not implement yet stay marked as proposed where they appear.

## Actor kinds and stored operation types (#174)

`registration_operation_audit` now records who performed each fact in `actor_kind`, which every writer must state (there is no default):

| `actor_kind` | `actor_user_id` and `session_id` | Used for                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------ | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `admin`      | both required                    | every admin command (manual registration, cash and complimentary confirmation, pass assignment, selection change, pass removal, registration void, name/AKA correction, discipline swap, cash correction) |
| `public`     | both null                        | a buyer without an account (no session exists)                                                                                                                                                            |
| `system`     | both null                        | server-side decisions such as a verified online payment                                                                                                                                                   |

Database checks reject any other kind and any mixed pairing, such as an admin fact without a session or a public fact with a user. In code, writers pass a typed actor (`{ kind: 'admin', userId, sessionId } | { kind: 'public' } | { kind: 'system' }`) and map it with `registrationAuditActorColumns` (`apps/backend/src/events/registration-audit/`). Facts written before this change are backfilled as `admin`.

Stored operation types are `manual_registration`, `cash_confirmation`, `pass_assignment`, `pass_selection_change` (an administrator replaces a held pass's competition selections; a `confirmed` registration requires an operator reason, stored in `note`, while a pending in-person sale needs none because nothing has been charged, like `pass_removal`, #170), `online_registration` (a public buyer creates a pending registration with its passes and selections), `payment_approval` (a verified online payment confirms a registration), `registration_expiry` (the system voids an online pending registration whose payment window ended, #181), `complimentary_confirmation` (an administrator confirms a $0 registration without payment, #263) and `pass_removal` (an administrator removes a held pass, with its selections, from an in-person registration still pending payment; no reason is required because nothing has been charged, #282), `registration_void` (an administrator voids a mistaken in-person registration still pending payment with a required reason, #104, migration `0033`), `registration_correction` (an administrator corrects the participant's name parts and AKA on a pending or confirmed registration with a required reason, #104, migration `0034`) and `registration_discipline_swap` (an administrator moves a registration's single full pass to another full pass of the same current price, with its new competitions and a required reason, #104, migration `0035`) and `cash_correction` (an administrator corrects the amount and/or paper receipt number of a confirmed cash payment after checking the original receipt, with a required reason, #104, migration `0037`). `amount_cents` is required for `cash_confirmation` and `payment_approval` and must be null for every other type, including `complimentary_confirmation`. `outcome` stays `accepted` only, and the immutability trigger still rejects every update and delete.

The `online_registration` fact shape is defined in the [public registration contract](public-registration.md#audit).

`payment_approval` is written by the Mercado Pago webhook (#177) in the same transaction that confirms the registration. Its actor is `system`; `amount_cents` holds the approved amount; `reference` holds the Mercado Pago payment id; `facts` holds only `providerPaymentId` and `checkoutId`; the before and after states record the pending to confirmed move with `approved_payment`, the confirmation time and the folio. It holds no payer data, card data, signature or provider payload. An approval for a registration that is no longer pending writes no fact; the webhook log records it as `approved_after_confirmation` or `approved_after_void` for manual follow-up (listed by the [flagged payments](flagged-payments.md) endpoint). The one exception is a late approval for a registration voided by `registration_expiry` (#181): when the event has not ended and the same person (email or phone) holds no other pending or confirmed registration in the event (expired ones are voided first), it is confirmed and its `payment_approval` fact has `before_state.status` `voided` and `facts.lateApproval` `true`. Otherwise the payment is recorded without a fact and the webhook log says `approved_after_expiry_duplicate` or `approved_after_expiry_event_ended`.

`registration_expiry` is written in the same transaction that voids the registration, by the purchase flow, the checkout or the periodic sweep. Its actor is `system`; `before_state` is `{ status: 'pending_payment', expiresAt }`, `after_state` is `{ status: 'voided' }` and `facts` is `{ reason: 'payment_window_expired' }`. Admin manual registrations have no expiry and never get this fact. Unlike the operator `registration_void` (#104), it needs no operator reason.

Public and system facts follow the same redaction rule as admin facts: no names, email, phone, raw session identifiers, payment instruments or provider payloads; reference the registration, participant and activity ids instead.

## Minimum durable operation fact

Each accepted material operation appends a distinct, immutable operation fact. Record a stable operation/fact id; operation type (`manual_registration`, `cash_confirmation`, `registration_correction`, `cash_correction`, `registration_void`); outcome (`accepted`, or a safe `rejected` decision when an attempted operation is auditable); server timestamp; actor kind, plus, for admin facts, the actor user id and server-side session record reference (never the cookie/token); authorized event id; event registration id; participant id; and the affected same-event activity ids (including added/removed ids where applicable). For creation, allocate the registration/participant ids within the write transaction so the accepted fact identifies created records. Capture a safe reason code plus operator-supplied reason where required; rejected facts must not disclose inaccessible target ids or sensitive inputs.

- `manual_registration`: Before: no event registration; after: new registration id, participant id, selected activity ids, `pending_payment`, null confirmation source/time. Record whether the participant was linked or created; no assertion of payment. Since #125, `facts.possibleDuplicatesConfirmed` lists the ids of the same-event registrations the administrator confirmed as possible duplicates (empty when there were none); it holds ids only, never names or contact data.
- `cash_confirmation`: Before: pending status and null confirmation metadata; after: `confirmed`, `admin_cash`, server confirmation timestamp. The original confirmation records the amount received, authenticated actor, and system timestamp (#58, delivered in #103), plus the required paper receipt number in `receipt`, unique within the event (#125; earlier rows without one are legacy); do not treat that amount as undecided. Since migration `0036_event_cash_payments` (#104) the same transaction also writes the registration's current cash payment (amount and receipt) to `event_cash_payments`, whose partial unique index on `(event_id, receipt)` reserves the receipt within the event; the fact keeps the values as confirmed. A later mistaken entry is a separate `cash_correction` case under #104, not a `registration_correction` field edit.
- `complimentary_confirmation` (#263, migration `0030_complimentary_confirmation`): Before: pending status and null confirmation metadata and folio; after: `confirmed`, `admin_complimentary`, server confirmation timestamp and folio. Written only when the registration holds at least one pass and its price snapshots total 0. The required operator reason is in `note`; `amount_cents`, `reference` and `receipt` are null, and no `event_cash_payments` row is written, so it cannot be cash-corrected. The reason is free text, so it follows the reason rule below.
- `pass_selection_change` (#170): Before/after hold the pass ids, price snapshot and selections; `affected_activity_ids` is the union of old and new selections. For a `confirmed` registration the operator reason is required (trimmed, 1 to 500 characters) and stored in `note`; a change without one is rejected and writes nothing. For a pending registration the reason is optional and `note` is null when none was sent. The reason is free text, so it follows the reason rule below.
- `registration_correction` (#104, migration `0034_registration_correction_audit`, name/AKA only): Admin actor, `amount_cents` null, `affected_activity_ids` `[]`. Before and after are both `{ status, participantId }`, so the unchanged status is evident; `facts` is `{ changedFields, otherEventCount, crossEventConfirmed }`, where `changedFields` lists the changed columns among `firstName`, `firstLastName`, `secondLastName` and `stageName`, `otherEventCount` is the number of other events where the same person holds a registration that is not voided, and `crossEventConfirmed` is true when that count was above 0 and the administrator confirmed. No old or new names are stored; the participant row holds only the current values. The operator reason is required (trimmed, 1 to 500 characters) and stored in `note`; a correction without one is rejected and writes nothing. The reason is free text, so it follows the reason rule below.
- `registration_discipline_swap` (#104, migration `0035_registration_discipline_swap_audit`): Admin actor, `amount_cents` null, `affected_activity_ids` the union of the old and new selections of the swapped pass. Before and after are both `{ status, passTypeId }` (the held and the new pass type), so the unchanged status is evident; `facts` is `{ fromPassTypeId, toPassTypeId, fromActivityIds, toActivityIds }`, where the activity ids are the pass's selections before and after (sorted). The registration pass keeps its id and its stored `price_cents` snapshot; no amount, checkout or payment fact is written. The operator reason is required (trimmed, 1 to 500 characters) and stored in `note`; a swap without one is rejected and writes nothing. The reason is free text, so it follows the reason rule below.
- `cash_correction` (#104, migration `0037_cash_correction_audit`): Admin actor, `amount_cents` null (the amount check of `0016` is unchanged, so `amount_cents` keeps meaning the money received at confirmation), `affected_activity_ids` `[]`. Before and after are both `{ status, amountCents, receipt }`, the previous and the new current values, so the unchanged `confirmed` status is evident; a legacy payment without a receipt has `receipt` null in `before_state`. `facts` is `{ changedFields, receiptChecked: true }`, where `changedFields` lists `amountCents` and/or `receipt`. The administrator must state that the original paper receipt was checked (`receiptChecked`); a correction without that statement or without the reason is rejected and writes nothing. The operator reason is required (trimmed, 1 to 500 characters) and stored in `note`; it is free text, so it follows the reason rule below. The new receipt must be unique among the event's cash payments. The current amount and receipt live in `event_cash_payments` (migration `0036`, backfilled from the `cash_confirmation` facts), which the correction updates in the same transaction; the audit stays the append-only history, so the original `cash_confirmation` fact keeps the amount, receipt, actor and time as confirmed, and every correction adds a fact with the values it replaced. `0036` dropped the `0029` audit receipt index (`registration_operation_audit_cash_receipt_uq`), since a corrected-away receipt stays in the history and must not stay reserved. This does not authorize a generic confirmed-state editor or void, provider-payment edits, refunds, or reconciliation.
- `registration_void` (#104, migration `0033_registration_void_audit`): Before: `{ status: 'pending_payment', confirmationSource: null, confirmedAt: null, participantId, activityIds }`; after: `{ status: 'voided', confirmationSource: null, confirmedAt: null }` per #61 row constraints. Admin actor, `amount_cents` null, `affected_activity_ids` the activities the registration gave access to before the void, `facts` `{}`. The operator reason is required (trimmed, 1 to 500 characters) and stored in `note`; a void without one is rejected and writes nothing. The reason is free text, so it follows the reason rule below. Passes and selections stay as history and prior facts stay in the append-only audit. Only in-person registrations (no `expires_at`) can be voided: an online pending registration expires on its own, and a later Mercado Pago payment on an operator-voided registration would land as `approved_after_void`, because only `registration_expiry` voids can be confirmed late. No confirmed-state void is authorized.

Safe snapshots are scoped to registration status, confirmation source/time, event/registration/participant/activity references, and the changed allowed registration fields; capture only what explains the decision. Do **not** store names, email, stage name, raw session identifiers/cookies, passwords, payment instruments/provider payloads, free-form customer data, or unredacted receipt images in operation facts. The name/AKA correction records only the changed field names (see `registration_correction` above), the discipline swap only pass type and activity ids, and the cash correction only the amount and receipt number; do not log whole participant rows or request bodies. Treat reasons as sensitive: constrain their content, redact secrets and personal details, and restrict readers. No public projection follows from these facts.

The authoritative registration mutation (including any participant/activity changes) and its required operation fact must commit in the **same database transaction**. Report success only after commit; if audit persistence fails, roll back the entire mutation. No successful unlogged material change. Enforce server-side active session, administrator role (not judge), event scope, and the **operation-specific** authorization decision before any mutation; reject duplicate, invalid, out-of-event, or unauthorized transitions without success. Audit safe rejection decisions where applicable without leaking protected targets; rejection never creates a successful state change. The [data lifecycle model](../models/data-lifecycle.md) remains the general append-only/redacted and atomic-write boundary; this contract does not alter it.

## Conservative correction and voiding boundary

- Creation is pending only. Cash confirmation is an explicit, one-time pending -> confirmed decision with `admin_cash` and `confirmed_at`; do not accept repeat confirmation, confirmation of voided/confirmed rows, or overwriting an `approved_payment` confirmation.
- Organizer-confirmed bounded scope is name/AKA and an equal-price discipline swap among Breaking, Popping, Locking, and Dancehall, with operator reason and safe before/after evidence. Keep registration and participant ids; validate same-event activity integrity and duplicate enrollment at write time. No folio edits, transfers, general-admission-to-participant upgrades, arbitrary identity changes, or payment-fact edits through `registration_correction`. The name/AKA correction is delivered (#104, 2026-10-21) for pending and confirmed registrations, online or in person; `voided` ones are rejected. There is no participant identity shared across events: each registration owns its participant row, so a correction changes only that registration and never other events. The cross-event count is informational: it counts other events where a registration that is not voided matches by the email/phone duplicate rule of manual registration, and when it is above 0 the write requires explicit confirmation (`confirmCrossEvent`). The equal-price discipline swap is delivered (#104, 2026-10-21) as `registration_discipline_swap`: a registration holding exactly one full pass (archived full pass types count) and no entry check-in (`event_check_ins` row), `pending_payment` or `confirmed`, online or in person (`voided` is rejected), moves that pass to another active full pass of the same event whose current `price_cents` equals the held pass type's current price. The registration pass keeps its id and its stored price snapshot (what was charged), so no difference is charged or refunded. The new pass's competitions are chosen in the same request (active `selectable` activities of the new pass type) and replace the old selections in the same transaction; add-ons and the registration row are untouched. Swaps for registrations with several full passes, swaps after entry check-in and swaps between passes of different prices are out of the MVP.
- Pending -> voided is permitted only as a justified error-void by an event-scoped administrator, for in-person registrations (delivered in #104 as `registration_void`); prior state stays in audit. `voided` is terminal for these commands (the system's late approval of an expired online registration, #181, is not one of them): no resurrection, correction, confirmation, or reuse as a refund. Paid cases go to the principal organizer outside automated MVP corrections; no confirmed -> voided command is authorized. Do not edit Mercado Pago payments or issue refunds.
- Cash correction is delivered (#104, 2026-10-21) as `cash_correction`: any event administrator corrects the amount and/or the paper receipt number of a `confirmed` registration that has a cash payment (an `event_cash_payments` row, written only by cash confirmation), after checking the original receipt (`receiptChecked`) and with a reason. Any amount above zero is accepted, as at confirmation, even when it differs from the passes' total; the new receipt (1 to 50 characters once trimmed) must be unique among the event's cash payments, compared exactly, and a legacy payment without one can get one. The registration's status, folio, passes and confirmation stay unchanged. Mercado Pago and complimentary confirmations, a `voided` registration and a correction that changes nothing are rejected. No attachments are stored. Cash recorded on the wrong registration, cash recorded without money received and a missing or disputed original receipt are out of the MVP: they go to the principal organizer outside the system. Do not infer confirmed -> voided, unconfirming, moving cash between registrations, a general confirmed-state edit, or a refund from this command.
- A confirmed registration's competition selections within a held pass may change only through `pass_selection_change` with an operator reason (#170). Passes, price, folio and payment facts stay unchanged; this is not a general confirmed-state edit.
- Reject cross-event registration/activity changes (distinct from a confirmed shared-identity name/AKA correction), duplicate activity links/registrations, arbitrary participant merges or deletes, and attempts to use correction/voiding as payment reversal, refund, or reconciliation. None of these operations changes payment-provider state or creates a receipt/settlement assertion.

## Registration history (#104)

Event administrators read their event's operation facts through `GET /admin/events/:eventId/registration-history` (delivered 2026-10-21; route details in the [operations contract](admin-registration-operations.md#registration-history-104)). The guard is `authorizeEventAdmin`: an active session with administrator authority over that event. Judges, administrators of other events and anonymous callers get the same safe 401, so a denial reveals nothing about the event. The read is read-only and changes nothing.

- Every operation type is listed, newest first by `(created_at, id)`. Migration `0038_registration_history_index` adds `registration_operation_audit_event_created_idx` on `(event_id, created_at DESC, id DESC)` for this read; the table had no secondary index since `0036` dropped the `0029` receipt index.
- Each item is a whitelisted projection: `id`, `occurredAt`, `operationType`, `actor { kind, name }`, `registration { id, folio, participantName }`, `reason` and `details`. `details` is built per operation type on the server (pass type and activity names resolved within the event, archived ones included; cash amounts and receipt numbers; the previous and new cash values of a correction; the approved amount and the late-approval flag of an online payment; status changes; the changed field names of a name/AKA correction), and `{ kind: 'other' }` for an unknown type. The stored `before_state`, `after_state` and `facts` are never returned as stored.
- `actor.name` is the administrator's `displayName`, or null when it is empty, and always null for `public` and `system` facts; the screen shows "Administrador", "Compra en línea" and "Sistema" for those. Never the email, the user id or the session.
- `reason` is the stored `note` only where it is the administrator's reason: `complimentary_confirmation`, `pass_selection_change`, `registration_void`, `registration_correction`, `registration_discipline_swap` and `cash_correction`. Reasons are shown to event administrators only and keep their 500-character limit. The optional `cash_confirmation` note is an operator remark at the till, not a reason, so it is not shown.
- The response holds no contact data (email, phone), session ids, user ids, Mercado Pago payment reference or checkout id, and no raw request bodies; the participant appears only by full name, with the folio when the registration has one.

Retention: nothing is deleted today. The immutability trigger of `0007` rejects every update and delete of `registration_operation_audit`, so facts are kept indefinitely, which exceeds the desired two years. The two-year period, subject to technical feasibility and legal review, and its mechanism are tracked in [#307](https://github.com/KevinBarrera/nuestro-breaking/issues/307); no compliance guarantee is established here.

## Release blockers requiring organizer/product decisions

Do not ship the relevant write command until its decision and enforceable policy are recorded:

1. **Authority:** all event-scoped administrators, never judges, may perform the agreed bounded corrections and read history in their authorized event. Define enforceable event-scope/access mechanics and command-specific guards for creation, cash confirmation, correction and pending void; do not reopen who may correct or read history. History access is delivered (#104, 2026-10-21): `authorizeEventAdmin` on the history route. Paid cases requiring payment reversal go to the principal organizer outside automated MVP.
2. **Fields and duplicates (#58):** the private registration inputs above are settled. Possible duplicates on email or phone require explicit admin confirmation, not an automatic block. Define implementation of those inputs, matching/presentation and confirmation mechanics, and the procedure for shared cross-event name/AKA edits without leakage; constrain discipline swaps to equal-price Breaking/Popping/Locking/Dancehall.
3. **Mistaken cash correction (#104):** settled and delivered (2026-10-21): the original receipt check and a reason, with the previous values in the audit and no attachments (see `cash_correction` above). Disputed or missing receipts, cash on the wrong registration and cash recorded without money stay outside the system with the principal organizer; no refund/reconciliation semantics.
4. **Corrections and retention:** reasons are trimmed, 1 to 500 characters, and shown to event administrators only; event-scoped history access and its redacted projection are delivered (#104, see **Registration history (#104)** above). The feasibility/legal review and mechanism of the desired two-year history remain open in [#307](https://github.com/KevinBarrera/nuestro-breaking/issues/307). Cash correction is delivered for the amount and receipt only; no blanket confirmed-state edit or void is authorized.

#102 read-only search can proceed independently. #103 delivered the original cash-confirmation amount/actor/timestamp boundary; #104 correction/void commands must consume the remaining policy decisions above before release. Payment-provider work, refunds/reconciliation, check-in, exports, UI, API commands, migrations, and role administration are outside #101.
