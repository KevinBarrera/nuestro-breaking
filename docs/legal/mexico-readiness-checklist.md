# Mexico Readiness Checklist

Planning checklist for counsel and operations before operating the event system in Mexico. It is not legal advice and does not establish legal compliance. Every item is pending until its owner records a review decision and supporting evidence.

## Data and Privacy

- [ ] **Pending:** Identify personal, sensitive, and operational data collected for accounts, registrations, roles, sessions, communications, and event operations.
- [ ] **Pending:** Obtain counsel's review of privacy notices, consent or other lawful bases, rights-request handling, and processor/vendor terms.
- [ ] **Pending:** Define approved retention, archival, deletion, and backup-deletion schedules by data category.
- [ ] **Pending:** Confirm security, access-control, incident-notification, and breach-response obligations.

## Participants and Events

- [ ] **Pending:** Define age classification, guardian consent, emergency contacts, and waivers for minors.
- [ ] **Pending:** Review participant releases, assumption-of-risk language, and accessibility or safeguarding responsibilities.
- [ ] **Pending:** Determine required handling, access, retention, and disclosure controls for incident and medical records.
- [ ] **Pending:** Obtain venue and local-authority guidance on permits, safety plans, capacity, insurance, noise, alcohol, and other event-specific requirements.

## Commercial Operations

- [ ] **Pending:** Review payment processing, chargebacks, taxes, invoicing, receipts, prizes, and payouts.
- [ ] **Pending:** Approve consumer-facing terms, registration terms, cancellation, refund, and dispute policies.
- [ ] **Pending:** Review contracts and allocation of responsibilities for sponsors, vendors, judges, staff, contractors, and volunteers.
- [ ] **Pending:** Define authority, approvals, records, and tax treatment for prizes and other payouts.

## Consent and International Operations

- [ ] **Pending:** Approve photography, video, livestream, testimonial, and marketing-consent flows, including minors and withdrawal handling.
- [ ] **Pending:** Identify cross-border guests, data storage, vendors, and transfers; obtain counsel's transfer and notice requirements.
- [ ] **Pending:** Review communications, marketing, cookies, and analytics practices against the approved privacy position.
