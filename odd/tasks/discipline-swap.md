# Swap a registration's full pass to another discipline

## Tracking

- GitHub issues: #104 (third slice: discipline swap) and #256 (its UI action)
- Branch: `feat/104-discipline-swap` → `dev` (base `8756848`), one commit per task. Split into chained PRs if the diff passes ~400 lines.

## Goal

An administrator moves a registration's only full pass to another full pass (Breaking, Popping, Locking, Dancehall) whose current price equals the current price of the held pass, choosing the competitions of the new pass and giving a mandatory reason. The pass change, the new selections and a `registration_discipline_swap` audit fact are written in one transaction. No amount, checkout or payment fact changes.

## Decisions

- **Scope (approved 2026-10-21, #104 comment):** equal-price full passes only, a registration holding exactly one full pass, no check-in. Multi-pass and post-check-in swaps are out of the MVP.
- **Check-in (2026-10-21):** the swap is rejected when the registration has an `event_check_ins` row (entry check-in), regardless of activity check-ins.
- **Competitions (2026-10-21):** the request carries the new pass's `activityIds`; each must be a `selectable` activity of the new pass type (`assertSelectable` rules). The old selections are replaced in the same transaction.
- **Same price (2026-10-21):** compare the current `price_cents` of both pass types. The registration pass keeps its stored `price_cents` snapshot (what was charged); no difference is charged or refunded.
- **Lifecycle (2026-10-21):** allowed on `pending_payment` and `confirmed`, online or in person; `voided` is rejected with 409.
- **Reason:** mandatory, 1 to 500 characters after trimming, stored in the audit `note`, as in the void and the name correction.
- **Audit redaction:** admin actor, no amount; before/after carry status and the pass type id; `facts` hold `{ fromPassTypeId, toPassTypeId, fromActivityIds, toActivityIds }`.
- **Out of scope:** several full passes, swaps after check-in, price differences, general passes and add-ons, cash correction, history view.
